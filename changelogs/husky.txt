DerpFest v17 (Android 17) for the Pixel 8 Pro (husky), build 20261024
Security patch: 2026-10-01 (system), 2026-08-05 (vendor). Stock Pixel firmware: bootloader-husky-ripcurrent-17.0-15681527.img + radio-husky-g5300i-260604-260807-b-16036236.img.

Notes:
==============================
- Unofficial build, signed with its own keys: updates from other ROMs (Evolution X included) don't install on it, and moving between ROMs needs a clean install.
- The update includes the stock Pixel firmware (bootloader and radio).
- Rooted? Before rebooting, install Magisk (or KernelSU) to the inactive slot, or root is gone after the update.
- Kernel: LineageOS's gs-6.1 kernel (Linux 6.1), built from source with the ROM.
- First installs need a computer: see https://xdaforums.com/t/4805779/

Device changes:
==============================
- allow OmniStyle's odex in /system
- keep Play services from preparing Google's OTAs
- turn NFC on by default, as stock does
- keep app odex on /system, not system_other
- point DerpFest's Updater at our own OTA repo
- DerpFest 17 product settings

ROM changes since the last build:
==============================
- Preinstalled apps keep their precompiled code across updates (app odex on /system, not system_other)
- The Contactless payments settings page that apps such as Google Wallet open loads again
- Play services' resume-on-reboot service is off, as on Evolution X (first unlock asked for the PIN twice there)
- Private Compute Services isn't restricted by LineageOS's 2022 allowlist anymore (stock dropped it)
- NFC on after a clean install, as on stock; PinnerService pins DerpFest's SystemUI
